Privacy Policy

Minimalist Inventory app · Last updated: May 21, 2026

This policy describes how the mobile application Minimalist Inventory (hereinafter, "the app") handles the information of the people who use it. The app is developed and maintained by Kain-Mark as an independent individual developer.

Quick summary: the app does not send or store your personal data on any server. Your inventory lives only on your device. The only information that leaves the device is what Google AdMob collects to show you ads, and that can be controlled from your phone's settings.

1. Data the app collects directly

None. All the information you enter in the app — inventory items, rooms, categories, notes — is stored exclusively in the local database on your device (SQLite). It is not sent to any server, not synced to any cloud, and not shared with third parties.

If you uninstall the app, all of that data is deleted with it. We do not keep backups of your information: we cannot recover it for you if you lose it.

2. Data Google AdMob collects

The app displays ads through the Google AdMob service (owned by Google LLC), integrated via the official Google Mobile Ads SDK. This SDK automatically collects certain information to personalize and measure those ads:

  • The device's Advertising ID.
  • Approximate IP address to infer your location at the city or region level.
  • Device information: model, operating system, system language.
  • Ad interactions: impressions, clicks, view duration.

We as developers do not have access to that raw data: we only see aggregated metrics (how many impressions were served, etc.). The full processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

3. System permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • Internet — needed only so that AdMob can download ads.
  • Network state — so AdMob doesn't try to load ads when there is no connection.

The app does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, calendar, external storage, or any other sensitive permission.

4. Cookies and local storage

The app does not use cookies. To store your inventory and remember whether you have seen the welcome screen, it uses Android's local storage (SQLite and SharedPreferences). These data never leave your device.

5. Minors

The app is not directed to children under 13. If you become aware that a child under 13 is using the app, please contact us and we will delete any related information to the extent possible (note that we do not collect data directly).

6. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you reside in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation grants you the following rights over any personal data processed in connection with the app:

  • Access to your data
  • Rectification of inaccurate data
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Objection to processing
  • Restriction of processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Because the app does not collect data on our servers, most of these rights are automatically exercised by uninstalling the app. For the data that AdMob collects, you must exercise them through your Google account settings. If you prefer not to see personalized ads, you can reset your Advertising ID from your Android device's settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID / Opt out of ad personalization.

7. California residents (CCPA)

If you reside in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act grants you the right to know what data is collected about you, to request its deletion, and to opt out of its "sale". We never sell personal data. For data processed by AdMob, please consult Google's policy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's functionality changes (for example, if a cloud backup feature is added in a future version). In that case, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top, and, if the change is substantial, we will announce it in the app description on Google Play.

9. Data deletion requests

As noted in section 1, the app does not collect or store your personal information on any of our servers. All your inventory data lives only on your device.

  • Local data (items, rooms, categories, preferences): completely removed when you uninstall the app. You can also clear it manually from Android Settings → Apps → Minimalist Inventory → Storage → Clear data.
  • Data processed by AdMob (Advertising ID, IP, ad metrics): you can request its deletion by contacting Google through their official form or by resetting your Advertising ID from your device's settings.
  • Direct request to the developer: if you want us to confirm that no data associated with you remains under our control, write to kainmark82@gmail.com with "Data deletion request" in the subject. We will reply within 30 days at most.
